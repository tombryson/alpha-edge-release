# Alpha Edge

Alpha Edge brings portfolio holdings, research, market evidence, signals and alert connections into one working view. Each page owns a distinct part of that decision flow.

## Guided Tour

The first visit to Positions offers a welcome and an optional guided tour.
Choose **Show me around** to follow Portfolio, Analysis, Positions, Alerts,
Markets, System and History. The guide sits above the workspace so the actual
pages remain available. You can move backwards, close it, or explore another
page at any time. **Help > Guided tour** opens it again.

Skipping, starting or completing the tour suppresses the welcome on later visits
in that browser. Demo and private modes remember this separately. Direct links
to other pages are not interrupted. The guide only navigates: it never changes
targets, confirms subscriptions, starts paid research or records an execution.

## Access

On a private deployment with owner sessions enabled, sign in using your passkey.
The shield in the header lets you add a backup passkey or sign out. Save the
offline recovery codes shown at enrollment; each can register a replacement
passkey once. Recovery replaces old access and issues new codes. Do not share
these codes or the private setup token.

The separate public **Demo** uses real ASX names with simulated portfolio data and cannot record trades, change
targets, import statements or run paid research. Your real portfolio is not part
of that demonstration. Older deployments may still use the API-token prompt
until their owner-access migration is completed.

## Start With What You Own

Connect the private application to its backend and import a complete supported broker statement. Signing in authorises access; it does not create holdings. External holdings from another broker remain manually maintained and are not verified by an IG statement.

Review [Positions](positions.md) for current holdings, [Portfolio](portfolio.md) for the approved asset-class mix and [Alerts](alerts.md) for TradingView setup. A watchlist item is research, not a holding.

## Three Different States

- **Held:** the broker evidence for what is currently owned.
- **Approved:** the class allocation you have agreed to implement.
- **Permitted now:** what current risk, trend, class capacity and cash rules allow.

Research suggestions can change without changing your approved shape. A new signal can change permitted action without proving a trade happened. You place orders at your broker and then record the execution in Alpha Edge.

## Find The Right Surface

The Alert Stack is for current work requiring a response. The Alerts tab is the connection ledger. History retains past signals, responses and statement evidence. The right sidebar provides optional security, ETF and shape context; it is not another approval queue.

## When Something Is Missing

Check the source date, connection setup and backend availability. Blank or unavailable data is not zero, a neutral trend or a completed trade. Do not repeat an execution entry or a paid AI submission simply because a result has not appeared.
